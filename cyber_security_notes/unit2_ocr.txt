Privilege Escalation Privilege escalation can be defined as an attack that involves gaining illicit (illegal) access Of elevated rights, or privileges. beyond What is intended or entitled for a user. This attack can involve an external threat actor or an insider. Privilege escalation is a key stage of the cyberattack chain and typically involves the exploitation of a privilege escalation vulnerability, such as a system bug. misconfiguration, or inadequate access controls. The attacker can use the newly obtained privileges to steal confidential data. run administrative commands or deploy malware — and potentially do serious damage to your operating system, server applications, organization, and reputation. In general, attackers exploit privilege escalation vulnerabilities in the initial attack phase to Override the limitations Of their initial user account in a system or application.
